About Rest Assured AFH

Care guided by professional nursing experience and family values.

Rest Assured AFH was created to offer a calm, respectful, and home-like alternative for families seeking dependable daily care. The home is led by a licensed nurse with advanced education and a deep commitment to serving older adults with patience, kindness, and professionalism.

Our approach is personal and relationship-based. We believe residents should feel known, safe, and valued — not simply cared for as part of a routine. Families can expect clear communication, thoughtful support, and a care environment shaped by dignity, compassion, and strong family values.

Services

Personalized support for daily living and peace of mind.

Services are tailored to each resident’s needs and are provided within the home’s licensed scope of care. Final availability should be confirmed during the admission conversation.

Personal Care

Assistance with bathing, grooming, dressing, toileting, mobility, and other activities of daily living.

Nurse-Led Support

Care guided by nursing knowledge, observation, communication, and attention to resident well-being.

Medication Assistance

Medication reminders and assistance according to care plans, provider instructions, and applicable regulations.

Memory & Dementia Care

Supportive routines, calm communication, and a safe environment for residents with memory-related needs, if accepted by the home.

Hospice & Comfort Support

Compassionate support for residents and families in coordination with hospice or outside healthcare providers, when appropriate.

Respite Care

Short-term stays may be available for families needing temporary support or caregiver relief.

Meals & Nutrition

Home-cooked meals, snacks, hydration support, and attention to individual preferences and dietary guidance.

Housekeeping & Laundry

Clean living spaces, laundry support, and daily routines that help residents feel comfortable and at home.

Admissions

Who may be a good fit?

Rest Assured AFH may be appropriate for adults or seniors who need help with daily living, medication routines, meals, mobility, supervision, or a quieter home setting than a larger facility.

  • Families looking for a smaller, more personal care environment
  • Residents who benefit from consistent routines and attentive caregivers
  • Individuals needing support with daily care, meals, and supervision
  • Short-term respite stays, if available

Please do not send detailed medical information by email. A full care discussion should take place privately during the admission and assessment process.
